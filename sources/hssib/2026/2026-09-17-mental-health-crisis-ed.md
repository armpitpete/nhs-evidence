# HSSIB — Mental health crisis care in emergency departments

## Factual capture

Published 17 September 2026.

HSSIB reports that emergency departments are often caring for people in mental-health crisis in environments not designed, equipped or resourced for therapeutic mental-health care. The investigation describes prolonged waits, overcrowding, limited privacy, unmet basic needs, workforce pressure and wider pathway gaps that can leave emergency departments acting as a default location.

The report groups findings around patient experience, workforce, oversight and decision-making, information sharing/systems, and interagency working.

## Limitations

These are national investigation findings. They do not establish that every emergency department has the same problems or that the findings apply unchanged to York or Scarborough.
