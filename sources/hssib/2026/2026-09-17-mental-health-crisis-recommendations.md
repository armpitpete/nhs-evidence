# HSSIB — mental-health crisis emergency-care safety recommendations

HSSIB published two national safety recommendations on 17 September 2026.

R/2026/097 recommends that DHSC/NHS England work with relevant national stakeholders on national minimum operational service standards and built-design expectations for emergency departments caring for people in mental-health crisis.

R/2026/098 recommends work on roles/accountability, shared-care and escalation arrangements, workforce capacity/capability/resilience, and agreed measures/reporting.

These are formal remedy recommendations. They are not evidence that DHSC/NHS England has accepted or promised to implement them.
