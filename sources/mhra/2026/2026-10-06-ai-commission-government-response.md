# Government response to National Commission on AI in healthcare

Published 6 October 2026.

## Factual capture

The UK government accepted all 44 recommendations of the National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare.

The response includes two observable implementation commitments: the MHRA intends to issue draft guidance by December 2026 on managing changes to adaptive AI-enabled medical devices; and a full implementation roadmap covering roles, responsibilities, timelines and key deliverables across all 44 recommendations is to be published by Spring 2027.

## Accountability significance

Acceptance is a baseline decision, not evidence that implementation is complete. The December guidance and Spring 2027 roadmap are future deliverables that can be observed directly.

## Limitations

"Spring 2027" is a seasonal target rather than a precise calendar date, so no exact roadmap deadline is invented.
