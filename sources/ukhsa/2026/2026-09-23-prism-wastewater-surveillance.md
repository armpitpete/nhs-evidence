# UKHSA PRISM surveillance expansion — 23 September 2026

## Factual capture

UKHSA announced an expansion of wastewater and environmental surveillance under the PRISM programme.

The announcement states that **£3 million** from the UK Integrated Security Fund will support expansion to hospital wastewater sites and other high-footfall locations.

UKHSA says PRISM builds on a wastewater-monitoring development programme launched in April 2025 and is intended to strengthen early detection and monitoring of pathogens with significant public-health threat.

## Limitations

This source establishes the programme announcement, scope and stated purpose. It does not establish how well the expanded system will perform in practice or what operational outcomes it will produce.
