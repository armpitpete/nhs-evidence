# ONS Death Certification Reform — 25 September 2026 capture

## Factual capture

ONS published the scheduled Death Certification Reform 2025 material on 25 September 2026. The release reports that 81.7% of 2025 deaths were doctor-certified and scrutinised by a medical examiner; uncertified/unknown registrations fell from 13,676 in 2023 to 4 in 2025; and median doctor-certified registration time increased from six days in 2023 to eight days in 2025.

The release set also includes the 2025 annual deaths bulletin and mortality datasets by ethnic group and pregnancy status.

## Limitations

This capture verifies publication and selected reported statistics. It does not establish that the reform has achieved all intended policy outcomes or evaluate medical-examiner-service effectiveness.
