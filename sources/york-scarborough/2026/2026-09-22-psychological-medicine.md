# York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals — Psychological Medicine

## Factual capture

The Trust states that its Psychological Medicine department is not a crisis service. For urgent mental-health help, its public information directs people to NHS 111 and the mental-health option, with regional crisis-service routing.

## Limitations

This page does not describe emergency-department mental-health liaison capacity, waiting times, admission pathways or outcomes.
