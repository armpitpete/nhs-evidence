# York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals — Martha's Rule

## Factual capture

The Trust's public patient information says Martha's Rule is available 24 hours a day for adult and paediatric inpatients at York and Scarborough hospitals. It describes a route for inpatients, relatives and carers to request review where they are concerned about deterioration and concerns remain unresolved.

## Limitations

The page describes inpatient availability. It does not establish that Martha's Rule is available in the Trust's emergency departments, nor does it publish local call volumes, response times or outcomes on this page.
