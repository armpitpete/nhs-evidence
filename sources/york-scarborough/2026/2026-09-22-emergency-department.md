# York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals — Emergency Department information

## Factual capture

The Trust says it operates emergency departments at York Hospital and Scarborough General Hospital. Its public information directs people with non-life-threatening conditions to alternatives including GP services and NHS 111, and includes mental-health problems among conditions for which people may first seek non-ED support when the situation is not an immediate emergency.

## Limitations

Public service-navigation information does not describe the actual pathway, waiting time, staffing or outcomes for people who do attend an emergency department in mental-health crisis.
