# NHS England — The Week — 22 September 2026

## Provenance

This record is a factual normalization of a user-supplied copy of the NHS England bulletin. The complete third-party bulletin is not republished in this public repository. No issue-specific original URL was present in the supplied copy.

## Complete factual capture

### Immediate / national items

- NHS England said it had written to trusts and integrated care boards about recommendations and immediate actions arising from the Thirlwall Inquiry, and said boards should urgently review the letter and follow up.
- The NHS-wide **Your Voice: shaping the future of maternity and neonatal care** conversation is open to staff. Feedback is intended to inform the National Maternity and Neonatal Taskforce action plan.
- A World Patient Safety Day film from the National Medical Director discusses safe care, the NHS Quality Strategy and the 10 Year Health Plan.
- Organ Donation Week runs 21–27 September. The bulletin states that more than 8,000 people are currently waiting for a transplant.
- A reformed National Cancer Board met for the first time, with the stated aim of strengthening national leadership on cancer diagnosis, treatment and care.

### Local / service news

- WELL Runcorn, in a former library, is described as a health, wellbeing and education hub within the £23.6m Reconnecting Runcorn programme funded through the Government's Towns Fund.
- A 29 September showcase was advertised for the public to see developments at Basildon, Southend and Broomfield hospitals.
- A Suffolk patient with keratoconus awaiting a cornea transplant was featured in an organ-donation story.
- London Ambulance Service reported 599,300 emergency calls from June to August, stated as 2.3% above its previous summer record in 2021.

### Guidance

- NHS England set out plans to implement **Martha's Rule in all emergency departments**, extending access to escalation support already available in adult and children's inpatient services.
- Guidance on implementing quality management systems includes an adaptable implementation plan for providers and commissioners.
- Guidance on achieving excellent patient experience describes expected behaviours, systems and culture and includes a self-assessment tool.

### Consultations

- Tobacco and vapes packaging, appearance and display consultation: stated closing time 23:59 on 2 October 2026.
- DHSC consultation on use of the professional title **nurse**: stated closing time 23:59 on 8 October 2026.
- The bulletin listed no newly opened consultations and no concluded consultation outcomes for that week.

### Events and engagement

- Communicating winter vaccines webinar: 5 October, 14:00–15:15; includes campaign performance, operational delivery and a section on mis/disinformation.
- Medical Education Training Review virtual engagement events: September and October.
- Co-production and quality-improvement webinar series: through December.
- Primary and secondary care staff were invited to user research on reporting and analytics for patient communications, involving a 60-minute interview and optional 60-minute group discussion; expression of interest requested before 31 October.
- NICE annual audience research survey: stated deadline 17:00 on 11 October.

### Partner / improvement material

- CQC publications were listed for South Western Ambulance Service NHS Foundation Trust and Wrightington, Wigan and Leigh Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust.
- East of England Community Health and Care NHS Trust was described as approved as a prospective advanced foundation trust.
- HQIP resources highlighted inequities in hip-fracture care, MBRRACE-UK maternal mortality/severe morbidity findings, and national cancer audit material covering primary and metastatic breast cancer, pancreatic cancer, non-Hodgkin lymphoma and oesophago-gastric cancer.

### Expected during the week

- Tuesday: national in-person CEO leadership event in London; Ian Paterson inquest continuing.
- Wednesday: CQC report on University Hospitals Sussex — Worthing Hospital.
- Thursday: DHSC adult carers survey 2025–26 and multiple NHS England statistical releases, including AAA screening, GP appointments, cloud telephony, diagnostic imaging, GP workforce, A&E activity, admitted patient care, outpatient activity, sickness absence, staff earnings, Talking Therapies, NHS workforce, PCN workforce, online consultation submissions and Federated Data Platform uptake/benefits.
- Thursday: UKHSA annual epidemiological commentary, COVER vaccination statistics, and national flu/COVID-19 surveillance for week ending 20 September 2026.
- Friday/weekend: CQC report on Portsmouth Hospitals University NHS Trust.
- Friday/weekend: ONS 2025 death-certification reform statistics, including mortality by ethnicity and mortality by pregnancy status.
