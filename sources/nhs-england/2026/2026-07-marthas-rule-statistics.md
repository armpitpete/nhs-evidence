# NHS England — Martha's Rule Programme July 2026

## Factual capture

NHS England reports 19,177 Martha's Rule escalation calls from September 2024 to July 2026. Of these, 5,660 related to acute deterioration. The published summary reports 749 acute-deterioration calls requiring transfer of care and 2,683 requiring other changes in treatment.

The publication says phase 3 commenced in April 2026 and expanded introduction into emergency departments, maternity and neonatal settings. Setting-level data collection began in June 2026.

## Limitations

The aggregate programme figures do not by themselves establish consistent availability, awareness, accessibility or outcomes in every setting or trust.
