# NHS England — Martha's Rule

## Factual capture

- NHS England describes Martha's Rule as a patient-safety initiative allowing patients, families and carers to request a rapid review when they are worried about deterioration and feel concerns are not being addressed.
- Acute inpatient implementation is still in progress and is expected to be complete during 2026/27.
- NHS England says the initiative is generally not yet available in maternity, neonatal and emergency-department settings, although phase 3 testing is under way in a smaller number of these services and in other settings.
- The NHS Standard Contract 2026/27 requires NHS trusts and foundation trusts to implement the three core components by 31 March 2027.
- NHS England says monthly data returns are being used to assess implementation and impact.

## Limitations

This page describes national implementation and programme intent. It does not establish that every emergency department currently offers Martha's Rule or that use and outcomes are uniform between organisations.
