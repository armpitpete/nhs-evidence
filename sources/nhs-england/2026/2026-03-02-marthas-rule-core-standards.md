# NHS England — Martha's Rule: core standards

## Factual capture

Published 2 March 2026.

The guidance sets out six core standards intended to support safe, effective and reliable implementation. It links implementation to Service Condition 33 of the NHS Standard Contract 2026/27, which requires NHS trusts and foundation trusts to implement the three core components of Martha's Rule by 31 March 2027.

The guidance is designed to support local assessment, commissioner assurance and inspection while allowing local adaptation of operational processes.

## Limitations

The existence of a national standard does not establish local compliance or effectiveness.
