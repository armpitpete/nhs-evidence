# NHS England — 2026/27 NHS Standard Contract, SC33.13

Service Condition 33.13 requires an NHS Trust or NHS Foundation Trust provider to implement the three core components of Martha's Rule by **31 March 2027**.

This establishes a testable contractual obligation and deadline. It does not establish completion or uniform implementation quality.
