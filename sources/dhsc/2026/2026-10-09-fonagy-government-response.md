# Government response to the Fonagy review

Published 9 October 2026.

## Factual capture

The government responded to the final independent review into mental health conditions, ADHD and autism with a stated move towards earlier, needs-led support while retaining diagnosis where clinically appropriate.

For autism and ADHD services, NHS England will consult on a national set of prices for NHS-funded services and intends to introduce finalised new payment arrangements from April 2027, covering assessment, post-diagnostic support and follow-ups.

The response also says government will work towards making all diagnostic assessments for ADHD and autism a Care Quality Commission-regulated activity and will set out further action in a cross-government mental health strategy later in 2026.

## Accountability significance

The April 2027 payment-arrangement statement is a concrete future implementation commitment.

## Limitations

The publication does not itself prove improved access, shorter waits, higher-quality diagnosis or better outcomes.
