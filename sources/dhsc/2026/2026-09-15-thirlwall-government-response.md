# DHSC — government response to the Thirlwall Inquiry

Published 15 September 2026.

## Factual capture

The government announced immediate work following the Thirlwall Inquiry, including work on neonatal cot-cam plans, insulin/medical-examiner guidance, NHS manager regulation and a single tracker for recommendations from major maternity and neonatal reviews/inquiries.

The Health and Social Care Secretary also stated that the government would consider the inquiry report in full and publish a **full response** after detailed consideration.

## Accountability significance

The full-response statement is a testable publication promise, although no specific publication date was given.

The announced immediate measures are evidence of work starting in identified areas; they are not evidence that the inquiry's recommendations have all been implemented.

## Limitations

No deadline for the full response is specified in this source. A later publication must be observed before the promise can be marked fulfilled.
