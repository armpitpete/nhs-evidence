# Mental-health crisis care: national ED findings versus the York pathway

## Signal

HSSIB has published national findings about prolonged waits, unsuitable emergency-department environments and gaps in the wider mental-health crisis pathway. York and Scarborough public pages describe routes through NHS 111 and state that Psychological Medicine is not a crisis service, but they do not expose what happens operationally when a person in crisis presents to ED.

## Why this may be worth investigating

This creates a bounded local question: whether York and Scarborough's actual emergency mental-health pathway shows, avoids or differs from the pressures HSSIB identified nationally.

It is not evidence that local services are failing.
