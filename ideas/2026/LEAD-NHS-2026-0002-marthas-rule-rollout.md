# Martha's Rule in emergency departments: rollout versus real use

## Signal

NHS England now provides a clear national standard, contractual deadline and phase-3 expansion into emergency departments. Its public overview also says Martha's Rule is generally not yet available in these settings.

York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals publishes 24/7 inpatient Martha's Rule access, but the captured local material does not establish emergency-department availability.

## Why this remains worth investigating

The original question has become more precise. The issue is no longer whether a national implementation standard exists; it does. The remaining question is how rapidly emergency-department availability becomes real, visible and measurable between sites.

This is not evidence that the rollout is ineffective.
