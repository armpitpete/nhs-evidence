# Martha's Rule in emergency departments: rollout versus real use

## Signal

The captured NHS England bulletin describes plans to implement Martha's Rule in all emergency departments.

## Why this may be worth investigating

A national rollout creates a measurable follow-up question: whether the escalation route becomes consistently available, understood and used in emergency departments, and what published evidence exists about its operation.

This is not evidence that the rollout is ineffective.

## Questions

- What is the implementation timetable?
- What counts as compliant implementation?
- How are patients and families told the escalation route exists?
- Will trusts publish usage, response or outcome data?
- Are there meaningful differences between trusts?
- Do complaints, patient-safety reports or evaluations identify barriers?

## Evidence that would strengthen the lead

- Published variation in implementation or use.
- Repeated reports of access barriers.
- Evaluation data showing substantial differences between sites.

## Evidence that would weaken the lead

- Consistent implementation evidence and accessible usage reporting.
- Evaluation showing the mechanism works similarly across settings.

## Limitations

The current source establishes an implementation plan, not completion, uptake or outcomes.
