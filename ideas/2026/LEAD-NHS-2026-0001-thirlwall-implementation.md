# Thirlwall actions: implementation and assurance gap

## Signal

NHS England's captured bulletin says trusts and ICBs were sent recommendations and immediate actions and that boards should urgently review and follow up.

## Why this may be worth investigating

The next useful question is not whether actions were announced, but how implementation is evidenced across organisations and how completion or non-completion is assured.

This is a **lead**, not evidence that any organisation has failed to implement the actions.

## Questions

- What exact actions and deadlines are in the underlying NHS England letter?
- What evidence must trusts and ICBs produce?
- Is there a central assurance or reporting mechanism?
- Are board papers or public updates showing implementation?
- Does later CQC or NHS England material test whether actions were completed?

## Evidence that would strengthen the lead

- Published implementation deadlines with incomplete or absent follow-up.
- Board papers showing uneven implementation.
- Regulator or NHS England findings identifying non-compliance or delay.

## Evidence that would weaken the lead

- Clear, timely published assurance showing widespread implementation.
- A robust national reporting mechanism with completion evidence.

## Limitations

The current repository record establishes that actions were requested; it does not establish any implementation failure.
