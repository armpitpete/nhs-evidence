# Public / Private Ingestion Boundary

This repository is public. Evidence capture must therefore apply data minimisation before commit.

## PUBLIC

Material already lawfully public may be recorded when relevant:

- official publications and datasets;
- public reports, board papers and consultation material;
- public court/inquest/PFD material;
- published journalism used with attribution;
- public FOI disclosures.

Preserve provenance and copyright discipline. Do not mirror complete third-party works without clear reuse rights.

## PRIVATE OR AUTHENTICATED CORRESPONDENCE

Emails, unpublished FOI correspondence, private messages and authenticated portal material are **not automatically publishable**.

Default treatment:

- store only the minimum metadata needed to preserve provenance;
- use a private reference, date, authority, case/reference number and factual outcome;
- do not commit full message bodies or attachments unless they have been deliberately cleared for public release.

## PERSONAL OR SENSITIVE INFORMATION

Do not automatically commit:

- private medical/health information;
- home addresses, personal phone numbers or private email addresses;
- information about children or vulnerable people that is not necessary for the public-interest evidence;
- confidential employment/personnel material;
- credentials, tokens, secrets or authentication artefacts.

Public-source personal information must still be minimised to what is necessary for the evidence.

## AMBIGUOUS CASES

If it is unclear whether material should be public, stop at metadata/reference level and require a human publication decision.

## Evidence integrity

Redaction or minimisation must not silently alter the meaning of evidence. Record that a source is privately held or redacted where necessary.

## Protected actions

The intelligence tasks may collect and organise evidence but do not themselves authorize:

- sending correspondence or FOI requests;
- publishing private material;
- purchasing access;
- deleting source evidence;
- merging protected `main`.

Those require their own authority.
