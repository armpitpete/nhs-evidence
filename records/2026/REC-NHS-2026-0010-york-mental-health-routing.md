# York and Scarborough public routing information for urgent mental-health needs

Public Trust information directs non-immediate mental-health needs away from the emergency department where appropriate and directs urgent mental-health help through NHS 111/regional crisis routing. Psychological Medicine is explicitly described as not being a crisis service.

## Limitation

The public pages do not establish how people who actually attend York or Scarborough emergency departments in mental-health crisis are managed, how long they wait, or what specialist support is available.
