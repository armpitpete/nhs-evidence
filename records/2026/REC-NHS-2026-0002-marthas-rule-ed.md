# Martha's Rule expansion to emergency departments

## Evidence

NHS England's bulletin says its letter sets out plans to implement Martha's Rule in all emergency departments, extending escalation support available in adult and children's inpatient services.

## What this establishes

- NHS England described an all-emergency-department implementation plan.
- The mechanism is framed as access to escalation support for patients, families, carers and staff.
- The bulletin does not establish implementation completion, utilisation levels or patient-outcome effects.

## Follow-up

Capture the underlying implementation letter, timetable, operational standard, coverage, usage statistics, local implementation evidence and evaluations.
