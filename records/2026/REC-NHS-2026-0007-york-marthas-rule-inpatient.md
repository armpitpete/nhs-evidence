# York and Scarborough publish Martha's Rule inpatient access

York and Scarborough Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust publicly states that Martha's Rule is available 24/7 for adult and paediatric inpatients at York and Scarborough hospitals.

## Limitation

The captured page establishes inpatient access only. It does not establish emergency-department availability, usage volumes or outcomes.
