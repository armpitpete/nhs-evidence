# Martha's Rule national implementation standard and phase 3 expansion

NHS England has published a defined implementation standard and a contractual deadline of 31 March 2027 for the three core components. Phase 3 extends introduction into emergency departments, maternity and neonatal settings, while NHS England's overview says those settings are generally not yet universally available.

Programme data through July 2026 shows substantial use overall and the start of setting-level reporting.

## Limitation

This evidence establishes programme standards, timetable and aggregate activity. It does not establish universal emergency-department implementation or equivalent local performance.
