# HSSIB remedy evidence — mental-health crisis care

## What this establishes

There is official recommendation-level evidence for national operational/design standards, clearer roles/accountability, shared-care and escalation arrangements, workforce planning, and agreed measures/reporting.

## What this does not establish

- that DHSC/NHS England has accepted the recommendations;
- that the recommendations have been implemented;
- that they will be sufficient to solve the problem;
- that York or Scarborough reproduces the national findings.

No government/NHS promise baseline should be created from these recommendations unless an authoritative response creates one.
