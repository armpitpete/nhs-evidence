# Thirlwall Inquiry: NHS organisational actions

## Evidence

NHS England's bulletin states that trusts and integrated care boards were sent recommendations and immediate actions connected with the Thirlwall Inquiry, and that boards should urgently review the letter and follow up.

## What this establishes

- NHS organisations were being asked to take immediate action.
- Trust and ICB boards were explicitly expected to review and follow up.
- The bulletin itself does not provide evidence here of which organisations had completed those actions or how implementation was being assured.

## Follow-up

Capture the underlying NHS England letter, implementation requirements, deadlines, assurance mechanism, trust/ICB responses and later inspection or outcome evidence.
