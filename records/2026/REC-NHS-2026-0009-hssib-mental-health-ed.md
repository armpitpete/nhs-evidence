# HSSIB identifies safety pressures in emergency mental-health crisis care

HSSIB reports national patient-safety problems associated with caring for people in mental-health crisis in emergency departments, including prolonged waits, unsuitable environments, pressure on staff and gaps across the wider urgent/emergency mental-health pathway.

## Limitation

The investigation is national and does not establish the same conditions at every local service.
