# NHS Accountability Review — monthly prototype template

Status: **prototype; first full window is October 2026**

## Purpose

Answer: **Across several weeks, what direction is NHS policy and delivery actually moving in?**

This is not four weekly reports concatenated.

## Sections

1. Commitments kept
2. Commitments slipping or repeatedly delayed
3. Commitments narrowed, expanded, superseded or withdrawn
4. Sustained changes in policy/implementation direction
5. Responsibility, funding or measurement changes
6. Recurring failures
7. Sustained improvements
8. National/local divergence
9. Remedies with accumulating evidence
10. Evidence gaps that persist
11. Strongest accountability questions for the next month

## Direction threshold

A sustained-direction claim requires multiple dated evidence points from more than one weekly window. Preserve incompatible definitions and counter-evidence rather than forcing a pattern.
