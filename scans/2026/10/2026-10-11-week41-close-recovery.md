### Mental health, ADHD and autism

On 9 October the Fonagy review final report and government response were published. The response sets a broad needs-led policy direction and includes a concrete NHS England commitment to consult on a national pricing approach and introduce finalised NHS-funded autism/ADHD payment arrangements from April 2027. That commitment is preserved as REC-NHS-2026-0020 and WATCH-NHS-2026-0006.

The policy response does not establish improved waiting times, diagnostic quality or outcomes.
