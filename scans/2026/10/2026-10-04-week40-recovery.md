# Recovered NHS evidence window — 2026-W40

## Purpose

This receipt reconstructs the missing Week-40 intake window from authoritative public sources after routine daily receipts were not durably captured. It records only directly supported observations; missing evidence is not treated as failure.

## Due-watch and accountability findings

### Thirlwall full government response — no supported transition

A bounded GOV.UK/DHSC check through 4 October found no full mapped government response to all 17 Thirlwall recommendations later than the 15 September immediate-actions response. The preserved full-response commitment therefore remains **announced/open**. This is NO CHANGE, not evidence of non-delivery.

### ONS weekly deaths — recurring release delivered

ONS published the scheduled weekly provisional deaths release on 30 September 2026 for week ending 18 September. The next release is listed for 7 October.

Source: https://www.ons.gov.uk/releases/deathsregisteredweeklyinenglandandwalesprovisionalweekending18september2026

Decision: recurring publication event observed as delivered; do not reinterpret it as unfinished delivery of the already-fulfilled September commitment.

### Leeds maternity and neonatal review — terms of reference published

DHSC published the independent Leeds maternity and neonatal review terms of reference on 28 September. Clinical case reviews are due to begin in November; the review is expected to conclude by 2029 and covers severe harm/death cases from 1 January 2011 through 31 March 2028.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/leeds-review-sets-out-terms-of-reference-for-investigation

Decision: material Yorkshire accountability development. This establishes review scope/timetable, not findings or remedy effectiveness.

### Healthy Babies / Best Start family hubs — implementation commitment expanded

DHSC announced on 30 September that Healthy Babies support will expand to all local authorities, with enhanced infant feeding and mental-health support alongside health visiting/midwifery support at more than 1,000 hubs by 2028.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/news/support-for-families-and-babies-rolled-out-to-all-family-hubs

Decision: new observable implementation commitment suitable for future accountability checking; announcement is not delivery proof.

### Cardiovascular disease modern service framework — published figures corrected

On 2 October DHSC/NHS England updated the CVD modern service framework and explicitly corrected modelled estimated impacts: “over 60,000 events avoided” became “over 57,000”; productivity gains £1.3bn became £1.2bn; health/social-care savings £1.2bn became £1.1bn. Two metric names were also corrected.

Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cardiovascular-disease-modern-service-framework

Decision: material evidence-quality correction. Preserve the earlier and corrected states; do not silently use the corrected figures as though the original publication never existed. The framework still states a 25% reduction ambition for premature heart-disease/stroke mortality within 10 years and says further implementation information will follow later in 2026.
