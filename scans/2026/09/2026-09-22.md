## Material changes

### Martha's Rule

The existing emergency-department rollout lead was advanced rather than duplicated.

New primary evidence establishes:

- national core standards;
- a contractual deadline of 31 March 2027 for the three core components;
- phase 3 expansion into emergency departments, maternity and neonatal settings;
- aggregate programme data through July 2026;
- published 24/7 inpatient availability at York and Scarborough hospitals.

The evidence does **not** establish universal ED availability. The lead therefore moves from `new` to `developing` and now focuses on actual ED coverage, use and variation.

### ONS release schedule

The ONS release calendar confirms the Death Certification Reform 2025 release for 25 September 2026 and provisional weekly deaths for 23 September. The existing release watch remains open because scheduled publication is not the same as observed publication.

### Mental-health crisis emergency care

HSSIB's 17 September report identifies national safety pressures around mental-health crisis care in emergency departments, including prolonged waits, unsuitable environments and wider pathway gaps.

York and Scarborough public information describes service routing but does not expose the operational experience of people who actually present to ED in crisis.

This created one new bounded local lead:

- LEAD-NHS-2026-0004 — Mental-health crisis care: national ED findings versus the York pathway

and one operational follow-up:

- WATCH-NHS-2026-0004 — York mental-health crisis emergency-care pathway follow-up

The lead explicitly does not infer local failure from the national findings.
