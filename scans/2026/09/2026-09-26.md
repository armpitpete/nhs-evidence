### ONS Death Certification Reform publications — 25 September releases observed

ONS published the scheduled Death Certification Reform material on 25 September 2026. The main article reports that 81.7% of 2025 deaths were doctor-certified and scrutinised by a medical examiner; uncertified/unknown registrations fell from 13,676 in 2023 to 4 in 2025; median doctor-certified registration time increased from six days in 2023 to eight days in 2025; and ONS says the timeliness change is not sufficient to materially affect the accuracy, interpretation or reliability of current death-registration statistics.

The same release set includes the 2025 annual deaths bulletin, mortality-by-ethnic-group dataset and mortality-by-pregnancy-status dataset.
