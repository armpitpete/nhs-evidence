# Thirlwall-related NHS actions — accountability timeline

## 15 September 2026 — government response baseline

The Department of Health and Social Care announced immediate work following the Thirlwall Inquiry and stated that the government would consider the inquiry report in full and publish a **full response** after detailed consideration.

Preserved evidence: `SRC-DHSC-THIRLWALL-RESPONSE-2026-09-15` and `REC-NHS-2026-0014`.

**Promise status:** announced.

No publication deadline is stated in the captured government source.

## 22 September 2026 — NHS organisational-action evidence

NHS England's bulletin states that trusts and ICBs were sent recommendations/immediate actions and that boards should urgently review and follow up.

Preserved evidence: `REC-NHS-2026-0001`.

This establishes an organisational-action obligation but the current source does not establish the full underlying requirements, completion deadline or assurance mechanism.

## What has changed

The repository now preserves two distinct accountability baselines rather than collapsing them:

1. NHS organisational review/follow-up actions;
2. the government's separate promise to publish a full response.

Immediate work announced by government does **not** satisfy the later full-response publication promise, and the NHS bulletin does **not** prove completion by trusts/ICBs.
